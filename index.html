Most people don't over-claim on purpose

They guess. A car claim that goes past the kilometre cap, a phone bill claimed twice because working from home already covers it, a tool claimed in full when it should be spread over years. The ATO compares your claims with people in similar jobs on similar incomes, so the guesses stand out.

DeductCheck applies the same rules a tax agent would. Where the rules say no, it tells you in plain words and shows you what would make the claim work next year.

Doing your own 2025–26 return?

If you lodge yourself in myGov, it's due by 31 October 2026. Check your work claims first, get a list of the records to keep, and enter figures that hold up.

The free check takes two minutes. If your claims are small enough that you don't need us, we'll say so.

How it works

  1. Take the free checkA few questions about your job and what you spent. You'll see whether the full report is likely to be worth it before you pay.
  2. Answer the full questionnaireOnly the sections that apply to you. Save and come back any time on the same device.
  3. Get your reportWhat you entered, what your answers support, the difference, and the reason for each gap. Warnings for claims that are likely to be questioned.
  4. Attach your receiptsAdd photos or files next to each claim. They're bundled into an evidence pack on your own device. We never receive copies.
  5. Enter the figures in myGovA step-by-step guide shows which supported figure goes where.
What we keep: your answers, your results and a record of which files you attached (a digital fingerprint, not the file). What you keep: the evidence pack. The ATO can ask to see records for up to five years, so store it somewhere safe.

What it covers

Car, travel, clothing and laundry, self-education, phone and internet, working from home, tools and equipment (including depreciation), union and professional fees, donations and the cost of managing your tax. It doesn't prepare your whole return. Rental properties, shares, crypto and business income are out of scope.
